### 1.	Project Title / Headline
Superstore Dashboard – Excel Reporting Project

### 2.	Short Description / Purpose
This is a beginner-friendly Excel dashboard project built on Superstore sales data. The goal of this project is to practice Excel skills like PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, and KPI cards, and to show sales, profit, and quantity data in an easy-to-read visual format.

This project helped me learn how to turn raw data into a clean, interactive dashboard that anyone can understand in a few seconds.

### 4.Process
Here is how I built this dashboard step by step:

1. Collected and cleaned the raw Superstore data in Excel.
2. Checked for blank cells, duplicate rows, and wrong data types.
3. Created PivotTables to summarize Sales, Profit, and Quantity.
4. Built PivotCharts (Pie Charts and Line Chart) to show data by Ship Mode, Segment, and Category.
5. Added KPI cards for Total Profit, Total Sales, and Total Quantity.
6. Added Slicers for Ship Mode, Segment, and Months so users can filter data easily.
7. Designed the dashboard layout with colors and labels for better readability.

## 7.  Project Insights (Key Findings)
Here are the main business insights I found from the dashboard:

*  **Sales**: ₹22.97L, **Profit**: only ₹5.98L — meaning profit margin is quite low, expenses may be high
*  **Quantity**: 37,873 items sold across the whole year
*  **Shipping**: Standard Class is used the most (60%+), because it's cheaper; Same Day is used the least (only 5%), because it's expensive
*  **Customers**: Consumer segment is the biggest (50%+), followed by Corporate (30%), and Home Office is the smallest (18%)
*  **Category**: Office Supplies gets the most orders; Technology and Furniture get fewer
*  **Sales Trend**: Sales start rising after August, peaking in Sep and Nov — likely due to festive/year-end shopping
*  **Profit vs Sales**: Even when sales rise, profit doesn't grow as much — meaning selling more isn't enough, margin matters too
*  **Best Product**: Binders is on top (₹1,07,242.76), followed by Machines, Phones, Copiers, Tables
*  **Worst Product**: Fasteners sells the least (only ₹1,015.91) — this item needs improvement

**🎯- Simple takeaway -**  The business earns good sales in the second half of the year, but profit growth is slow. Focusing on low-performing sub-categories like Fasteners and improving profit margins (not just sales volume) could help the business grow better.
